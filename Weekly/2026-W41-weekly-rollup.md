## What This Means for You

Julie, this is the week to get hands-on with MCP servers if you haven't already. Pick one external service you use regularly—a database, API, or data source—and build a simple server to connect it to Claude. You'll learn more by doing than by reading, and you'll have a reusable template for the next three projects. Also pay attention to the prompt structuring patterns—they're directly applicable to whatever you're building, whether that's automating customer support or processing documents. The gap between "works sometimes" and "works reliably" is usually better prompts plus thoughtful error handling, not more code.
